LOAF ADMIN PASSWORD AND ANIMATION GUIDE

CHANGE PASSWORD
1. Open /dashboard/ and sign in.
2. Open Security.
3. Enter the new password twice. Use at least 12 characters.
4. Select Change password.
5. Sign out and test the new password before closing the current session.

PC ANIMATIONS
- Hard refresh: Ctrl + Shift + R.
- Make sure system accessibility settings are not removing animations.
- Enable graphics acceleration in Chrome, Edge or Brave and relaunch.
- Temporarily disable extensions that alter CSS, dark mode, motion or performance.
- Turn off battery saver while testing.

IPHONE / IPAD / ANDROID
- Turn off Reduce Motion or Remove animations when the full experience is desired.
- Close and reopen the browser tab after deployment.
- Keep the browser updated.

The site respects prefers-reduced-motion. When the device requests reduced motion, decorative effects are intentionally simplified.
