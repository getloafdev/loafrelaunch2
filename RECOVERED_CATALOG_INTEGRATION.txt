Removed from the storefront
----------------------------
Invented placeholder names such as Banana Field, Violet Terrain, Jungle Cut, Glacier,
Noir Wave, Coastline, Deep Dunes, Ember, Ink Depth and Peach Strata are no longer used.
Placeholder records remain inactive in the database so old uploads are not destroyed.

Inventory safety
----------------
Recovered products begin out of stock. Set the real stock quantity and enable Available
to buy in Dashboard > Products before accepting orders.
