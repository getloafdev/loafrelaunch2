Quick answers

Before you choose your LOAF

Which size is better for students, A4 or A5?

A5 is compact and easier to carry every day. A4 gives more room for detailed class notes, assignments, diagrams and art.

Which page type should I choose?

Choose lined for traditional notes, dotted for flexible planning and diagrams, or plain for drawing and unrestricted work.

Are LOAF notebooks made in Bangladesh?

Yes. LOAF was established in 2020 and its premium notebooks and sketchbooks are designed and made in Bangladesh.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes. Cash on delivery is available for orders across Bangladesh.
